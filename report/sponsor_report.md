# Benefits realisation report: Student Services Transformation Programme

**For:** Programme Board and senior sponsors · **Data to:** September 2026 · **Prepared by:** Gana Cariappa

## Summary
The programme is delivering its cashable benefits: £15.4K saved in September (97% of plan) and £21.9K net to date. However, 4 of 9 measured benefits are behind plan. The lag sits mainly in quality benefits, which are held back by adoption and measurement rather than by the changes themselves, and in Automated Timetabling, which carries 3 of the 7 high risks. **The board is asked to approve a timetabling recovery plan, to be delivered by the end of December.**

## Key findings
1. **Efficiency benefits are landing; experience benefits are not.** 4 of 9 measured benefits are Red. All three quality benefits are behind plan: room utilisation (48% of planned improvement), student satisfaction (63%) and marking turnaround (67%). Cost and time benefits are Green or Amber.
2. **Cashable savings are on plan.** The programme saved £15.4K in September, 97% of plan. Net benefit to date is £21.9K after £18.8K of new portal licence and support costs.
3. **Delivery risk is concentrated in Automated Timetabling.** It holds 3 of the 7 high open risks, including both scored 16, and its manual-override workload is over tolerance (83 vs 80 hours a month).

## Risks and issues
- **Reporting gaps limit confidence.** 4 monthly reports are missing and 4 were late (up to 30 days). B08's RAG status rests on August data.
- **One benefit can't be measured.** HR administration cost (B12) has no agreed baseline or target.
- **The override dis-benefit is rising** and is linked to a high risk: the rules engine doesn't handle part-time staff constraints.

## Recommendations
Prioritised by how far each benefit is behind plan and the risks attached.

1. **Timetabling recovery plan, by 31 December 2026.** *Owner: Chief Operating Officer (sponsor), with the Head of Timetabling and the Finance Business Partner.*
   - Release the saved timetabling admin budget, so the cashable saving is actually banked.
   - Fix the rules engine to handle part-time staff constraints, bringing manual overrides back under the 80-hour tolerance.
   - Require all room bookings to go through the system, so true utilisation becomes visible.

2. **Unblock the quality benefits, by 31 January 2027.** Timed to allow training and survey activity around the holiday period.
   - Train markers on online marking tools. *Owner: Vice-Dean (Education).*
   - Add a one-question rating after each enquiry to raise survey response rates to a reliable level (target: at least 30%). *Owner: Head of Student Enquiries.*

3. **Strengthen benefits reporting.**
   - Chase the 4 missing reports at the next reporting deadline, 10 December 2026. *Owner: each benefit owner, coordinated by the Transformation Office.*
   - By March 2027, before the new financial year: agree a baseline and target for HR administration cost (*owner: Director of HR*), and introduce reminders and escalation for late reports.
